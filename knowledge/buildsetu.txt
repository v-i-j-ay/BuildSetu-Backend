BuildSetu is a platform that connects skilled labourers,
contractors and suppliers with people who need construction
and related services.

Electricians install, repair and maintain electrical wiring,
switches, sockets, fans and other electrical systems.

Before hiring an electrician, users should check previous
experience, safety knowledge, availability and expected charges.

Plumbers install and repair water pipelines, taps, sinks and
other plumbing systems. They can help with water leakage,
damaged pipes and plumbing maintenance.

Carpenters work with wood and can build or repair doors,
windows, furniture and other wooden structures.

Painters prepare surfaces and apply paint to buildings,
walls and other structures.

Users should verify a worker's experience and discuss the
work requirements, location, expected charges and availability
before hiring.
